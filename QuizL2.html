Phase A: place your 1s on the K-Map. Phase B: build your groups — switch to group-select mode, click cells (dashed outline), then Add group. Finish with the minimum possible number of groups, just like the lecture grouped m5+m7 and m7+m6 for the fan.

Rules of grouping (from lecture)

  • Only 1-cells may be inside a group
  • Group sizes: powers of two (1, 2, 4, 8)
  • Rectangles only — diagonals never count
  • The map wraps: left edge ↔ right edge are neighbors
  • Overlapping groups are allowed — the fan's m7 sat in both groups
  • Bigger groups → more variables cancel → simpler terms

⚠️ The checker verifies your groups against your chip. A classmate's screenshot will not help you.
